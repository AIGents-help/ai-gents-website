Our method

Field to Flow

A practical way to turn the work people actually do into a process they can explain, improve, and support with the right tools.

The method

See it. Shape it. Put it to work.

Field

Interview the people doing the work. Review the forms, tools, handoffs, decisions, exceptions, and bottlenecks.

Flow

Agree on a simpler path: who does what, what information is needed, where approvals happen, and how the result is checked.

Put it to work

Create the documents and digital tools, test the process with users, and make ownership and future updates clear.

Choose your level of support

Three ways to use Field to Flow.

Guided assessment

We interview the people involved, review existing forms and tools, and map one process. You receive a clear picture of the friction and a recommended first change.

Consulting engagement

Your team does the work with our guidance. We facilitate working sessions, review drafts, and help turn decisions into a usable workflow and document set.

Done-for-you implementation

We design and build the agreed workflow, documents, site or portal, connect suitable services, test it with users, and provide handover materials. Scope depends on the system.
