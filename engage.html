Fit

We work with a small number of organizations at a time. This is intentional.

We're the right fit when you areA serious buyer with real stakes.

  • A mid-market or enterprise organization ($50M+ revenue) with a genuine AI mandate
  • A leadership team that has budget authority and is ready to make decisions
  • Willing to be honest about where you are — not where you'd like to appear to be
  • Looking for a partner who will tell you what's true, not what's comfortable
  • Operating in a regulated or competitive-moat industry where AI has real stakes
  • Ready to begin within 30–60 days, not "exploring for future budget"

We're probably not the right fit if you needA different kind of firm.

  • A large team staffed with junior consultants billed at senior rates
  • Reassurance that your current AI strategy is correct (we'll tell you if it isn't)

  • Body-shop implementation at the lowest possible rate
  • A firm that will tell you AI can solve problems it cannot
  • Someone to manage an existing vendor relationship you've already committed to
A note on how we
price and structure
our work.

We don't bill by the hour and we don't staff projects with people you won't meet in the briefing. When you engage AI Gents, you get Vijayalakshmi's direct involvement — not a team of analysts she supervises from a distance.
