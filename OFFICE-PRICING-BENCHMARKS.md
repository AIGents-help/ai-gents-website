# AI Gents — Pricing Benchmarks and Adjustable Client Quotes

## Pricing rule
The 45 standard components retain their non-editable AI Gents benchmark starting estimates in `js/office-component-pricing.js`. The actual negotiated client price is a separate `ag_component_pricing` record per project component.

These are **industry-informed AI Gents benchmarks**, not objectively established fixed industry rates. Do not represent them to clients as independently certified standard prices or as guaranteed savings.

## Owner workflow
1. Check the requested project components.
2. Expand a selected component → Fees. The benchmark reference is displayed next to editable setup/monthly prices.
3. Adjust the client's setup fee, monthly fee, third-party estimate and quantity. The original benchmark reference stays visible.
4. To return to the catalog starting figures, choose **Restore benchmark**, then **Save quoted fees**. Restoring does not overwrite amounts until explicitly saved.
5. The internal estimate shows each item's benchmark and quoted price, plus reference and quoted totals; any difference is accurately labeled above/below benchmark.
6. To include benchmarks when copying an estimate, deliberately check **Include benchmark reference in copied estimate**. Without it, the copied estimate includes actual quoted amounts only.
7. Custom features have no reference figure and are explicitly labeled unbenchmarked.

## Commercial safeguards
- Benchmark comparison never changes a saved quote, invoice, approved scope or pricing record.
- Published/frozen scope versions keep negotiated prices and require a new version for changed terms.
- Discounts/scope overlap require human judgment; benchmarks are not evidence of a third-party market offer.
- Quote comparison, negotiation amounts and internal draft estimates are visible only to privileged Office staff. Assigned contractors and customers cannot view the private pricing table.
- No new accounts, vendors or paid subscriptions are created.
